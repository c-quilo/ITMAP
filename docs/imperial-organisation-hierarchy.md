## Directory rules

- Faculty names are navigation headings, not child units.
- Units represented by three or more ITMAP researchers appear in the browse directory.
- Smaller units remain available through exact-name search.
- A centre may have both a faculty group and a department host.
- Unrecognised future units fall into `Other Imperial units` until checked against an official source.
